\chapter{Short Answers}
\label{app:answers}

This appendix answers the parts of the exercises that you work on paper: the truth tables, the
equations, the traces, the numbers, and the questions whose answer is a sentence or two. It gives
the answer and no more, so that an answer worked on paper can be checked on paper; how to reach it
is in the chapter the exercise follows.

Two kinds of part are not here. \textbf{The VHDL} you are asked to write is checked by the
testbench the exercise ships, and its reference solutions are published in the companion
repository, under \file{lectures/LNN/solutions}. \textbf{The CircuitVerse builds} are checked by
simulating them against a truth table or a trace you already have; where a part asks you to
predict what the simulation will show, the prediction is answered here.

An entry is headed by the exercise it answers, with the page the exercise is on; the letters are
its parts. A part that is not listed has nothing to answer on paper.
